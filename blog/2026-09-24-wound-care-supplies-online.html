How to Choose the Right Wound Care Supplies

Selecting appropriate wound care products depends on several factors. Consider the wound type, size, depth, and drainage level. Different wounds require different types of dressings:

  • Dry wounds: Require moist dressings like hydrogels or hydrocolloids to prevent further drying
  • Heavily draining wounds: Need highly absorbent dressings such as foam or alginate products
  • Shallow wounds: Often heal well with basic sterile dressings and antiseptics
  • Deep wounds: May require specialized packing materials and advanced dressings
  • Infected wounds: Need antimicrobial dressings and appropriate cleansing solutions
