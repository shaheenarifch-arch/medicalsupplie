Frequently Asked Questions

What is MedicalSupplie?

MedicalSupplie is an independent buying guide. We research medical supplies, PPE, foot-health products, wellness devices, eyewear and U.S. telehealth services, then link you to trusted partner stores. We don't sell or ship products ourselves.

Do you earn money from the links?

Yes. Most partner links are affiliate links, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the price you pay. Read our affiliate disclosure.

Which gloves are best for everyday clinical use?

For most clinics and home carers, powder-free nitrile exam gloves around 3–4 mil thick are the best all-rounder: latex-free, puncture-resistant and comfortable. Choose 6–8 mil textured nitrile for cleaning or heavy-duty work. See our glove guide.

What is the difference between N95, KN95 and KF94 masks?

All three are filtering respirators rated for about 94–95% filtration of small particles. N95 is the U.S. NIOSH standard, KN95 is China's GB2626 standard and KF94 is South Korea's standard. Fit matters most. Details are in our mask guide.

Is telehealth safe for weight loss or hormone therapy?

Reputable telehealth services use licensed U.S. clinicians who review your history, may order labs and decide whether a treatment is appropriate. Ask about clinician licensing, follow-up and total costs before you start. Read what to expect.

Can arch-support insoles help plantar fasciitis?

Supportive footwear and orthotic insoles are among the first-line self-care options for heel pain, alongside stretching and rest. Persistent pain should be checked by a clinician. See our insole guide.
