About

Shaheen Arif is the founder of getservices.ai and the editor of MedicalSupplie, publishing buying guides about home medical supplies. Shaheen has more than 10 years of experience in IT and project management.

How our guides are made

  • Picks are based on published retailer product information, practical buying criteria and use cases.
  • We do not invent ratings, test results, prices or claims, and we tell readers what to verify on the product page.
  • Guides link to independent sources where safety, rules or health are involved.

What Shaheen covers on MedicalSupplie

Guides on MedicalSupplie focus on home medical supplies, written for US caregivers buying home medical supplies.
